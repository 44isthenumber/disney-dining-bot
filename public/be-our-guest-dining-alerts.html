Questions

Will you hold a Be Our Guest table for me?

No. We watch and text. You book on Disney’s site, on your own account. We do not guarantee a table will open.

Do you text every Be Our Guest opening?

No. You get a text only when a time that matches your watch newly opens.

Is this an official Disney product?

No. Magic Table Finder is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company.
