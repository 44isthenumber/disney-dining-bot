Magic Table Finder is a personal reservation alert service that monitors selected Disney dining criteria and sends alerts when matching reservation openings are found.

SMS Alert Program

Program name: Magic Table Finder Disney dining reservation alerts.

Users opt in by signing in at https://magictablefinder.com/, creating a watch on the website, and actively checking the unchecked SMS consent box before submitting the watch. The watch cannot be created unless SMS consent is checked. The public opt-in workflow is documented at SMS Consent Flow.

Messages may include reservation opening details and a direct booking link. Message frequency varies based on your watch activity and Disney reservation availability. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt Out and Help

You can cancel SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP. You may also delete watches in the Magic Table Finder website.

Payments

Paid watches are billed through Stripe. Completing Stripe checkout is not SMS consent. SMS consent is collected only with the unchecked checkbox on Create Watch. You can cancel a Planner subscription at period end through the Stripe billing portal. Reply STOP to opt out of texts even if a watch is paid.

No Guarantee of Availability

Magic Table Finder can alert you when it detects matching openings, but it does not guarantee that a reservation will remain available or that Disney will complete a booking.

Privacy

Our Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used. Mobile opt-in data and SMS consent will not be shared, sold, rented, or transferred to third parties, affiliates, or lead generators for marketing or promotional purposes.

Acceptable Use

Use the service only for personal reservation watching. Do not misuse, overload, resell, or interfere with the service.

Contact

For support, reply HELP to an SMS alert or contact the Magic Table Finder administrator through the website owner.
