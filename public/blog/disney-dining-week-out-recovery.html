Your hard Disney dining table is gone a week out — what to do next

September 22, 2026

You’re a week (or two) from Disney World and the hard table is still empty — Space 220, Cinderella’s Royal Table, ‘Ohana, same story. Missing the 60-day window, or losing an ADR to a cancel, does not mean the trip has no path left.

What still frees seats

Most table-service restaurants let guests cancel or modify up to about two hours before the meal (planDisney). When plans change, those seats can reappear. Planning guides and guest reports keep pointing at the final 48 hours — and sometimes the day of — as when people actually look again. That is a reason to keep checking. It is not a promise a specific restaurant will open for your party.

Some venues use stricter cancel rules (dinner shows and select fine dining show up in guides). Always read the cancel terms on the reservation you want.

What to do yourself first

  1. Search My Disney Experience for your true party size and dates. Try other meal times on the same day.
  2. Book a solid backup ADR so dinner is not hanging on one marquee name.
  3. If you’re already in the park, check whether that restaurant offers a walk-up waitlist in the app (Check Dining Availability → Hungry Now?). Walk-up helps at some spots; it is not a reliable bet for every hard table.
