Questions

Do you text every open table?

No. You get a text only when a time that matches your watch newly opens. Times that were already open stay quiet.

Is this an official Disney product?

No. Magic Table Finder is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company.

Do you book the table for me?

No. We watch and text a booking link. You reserve on Disney’s site with your own account.

What if nothing opens?

You wait. We keep checking. We do not guarantee a table will open.

How much does it cost?

Single Watch is $4.99 one-time. Planner is $14.99/month and includes up to 4 active watches.
