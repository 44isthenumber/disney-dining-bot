Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Magic Table Finder helps opted-in users monitor Disney dining reservation availability and receive alerts when matching openings are found.

Information We Collect

We collect the information needed to run your watches, such as your email address (to send a sign-in link and identify your account), your selected restaurant, dates, party size, meal periods, time window, and mobile phone number for SMS alerts.

How We Use Information

We use your email to send a sign-in link and to identify your account. We use your other information only to manage reservation watches, check availability, send requested SMS alerts, support opt-out/help requests, and maintain service reliability.

Signing in with email and saving a phone number on your account are not SMS consent. SMS consent is collected only when you create a watch, using the unchecked consent checkbox on that form.

SMS Consent and Mobile Data

Users opt in only by creating a Disney dining watch and actively checking the unchecked SMS consent box before submitting the watch. Signing in with email or saving a phone number does not constitute SMS consent. A watch cannot be created unless SMS consent is checked (when watch creation is available).

By creating a watch and checking the SMS consent box, you agree to receive SMS alerts from Magic Table Finder when matching Disney dining reservation openings are found. Message frequency varies based on your watch activity and reservation availability. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Mobile opt-in data and SMS consent will not be shared, sold, rented, or transferred to third parties, affiliates, or lead generators for marketing or promotional purposes.

The public SMS opt-in workflow is documented at SMS Consent Flow.

Service Providers

We use service providers such as hosting, storage, SMS delivery, and payment processing (Stripe) only to operate Magic Table Finder. They may process information solely to provide the requested service. Stripe processes payment card details; we do not store your full card number. Paying through Stripe is not SMS consent.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your mobile number or SMS consent for third-party marketing.

Opt Out

You can stop SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help.

Contact

For questions, contact the Magic Table Finder administrator through the website owner.
