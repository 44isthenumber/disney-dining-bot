Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Magic Table Finder helps opted-in users monitor Disney dining reservation availability and receive alerts when matching openings are found.

Information We Collect

We collect the information needed to run your watches, such as your selected profile, restaurant, dates, party size, meal periods, time window, and mobile phone number for SMS alerts.

How We Use Information

We use your information only to manage reservation watches, check availability, send requested SMS alerts, support opt-out/help requests, and maintain service reliability.

SMS Consent and Mobile Data

By creating a watch and checking the SMS consent box, you agree to receive SMS alerts from Magic Table Finder when matching Disney dining reservation openings are found. Message frequency varies based on your watch activity and reservation availability. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Mobile opt-in data and SMS consent will not be shared, sold, rented, or transferred to third parties, affiliates, or lead generators for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service Providers

We use service providers such as hosting, storage, and SMS delivery vendors only to operate Magic Table Finder. They may process information solely to provide the requested service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your mobile number or SMS consent for third-party marketing.

Opt Out

You can stop SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help.

Contact

For questions, contact the Magic Table Finder administrator through the website owner.
