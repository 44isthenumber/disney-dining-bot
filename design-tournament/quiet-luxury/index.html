Clarity

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on our alert semantics and privacy.

1. Do you text every open table?

No. You only receive an SMS when a newly opened reservation matches your exact restaurant, party size, and dining window. Times that have been continuously open stay quiet.

2. Whose phone gets the alert?

Alerts are delivered exclusively to the phone number associated with the signed-in profile (Craig or Jessica). Each profile maintains its own distinct watches.

3. Is this an official Disney product?

No. Magic Table Finder is an independent, private alert utility created for personal trip planning and is not affiliated with, authorized, or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company.

4. How often do you check?

Our service performs a calm check every 10 minutes around the clock, evaluating Disney dining openings against your active criteria.

5. When do you ask for SMS consent?

We ask for explicit, unchecked SMS consent at the exact moment you create a watch, not when you sign in. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.

6. What if nothing opens?

We continue watching quietly in the background. We never send empty messages or noisy status updates, and we do not guarantee a table if no opening is released.
