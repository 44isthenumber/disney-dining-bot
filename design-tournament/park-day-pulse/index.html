Straight answers

Before you set a watch.

Do you text every open table?

No. We send an SMS only when a time that matches your watch newly opens. A time that stays continuously open stays quiet.

Whose phone gets the alert?

The signed-in profile’s phone. Craig’s watches alert Craig; Jessica’s watches alert Jessica.

Is this an official Disney product?

No. Magic Table Finder is an independent dining alert tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Disney.

How often do you check?

Every 10 minutes. That rhythm keeps your watch current without turning the experience into an availability dashboard.

When do you ask for SMS consent?

When you create a watch. The SMS consent checkbox starts unchecked and must be selected before the watch can be created—not when you sign in.

What if nothing opens?

You wait, and we keep checking. Magic Table Finder cannot guarantee that a table will open or that you will be able to book it.
