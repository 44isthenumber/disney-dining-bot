# Team packet — Trusted Watch

**Slug:** `trusted-watch`  
**Branch:** `cursor/vision-trusted-watch-1361`  
**Folder:** `design-tournament/trusted-watch/`

Read [`../BRIEF.md`](../BRIEF.md) and [`../RUBRIC.md`](../RUBRIC.md) first. Honor every locked constraint. This packet is the **only** art direction you may use.

## Bet

Precision and honesty. Health + owner clarity as the trust engine.

The guest has been burned by flaky bots and fan-site dashboards. They will trust a product that shows exactly what is watched, who gets the text, and whether the checker is actually running.

## Voice

- Plain language from the product: Watch, Alert me, Party, Date, Time window, Any meal, Last checked.
- Prefer: “Craig’s watches go to Craig’s phone.” / “We only text when a matching time newly opens.”
- Avoid: luxury serif poetry and hype lime. No “magic” as a fireworks word; the product name is enough.
- Headline direction (you may rewrite, keep the temperature):  
  **Know it’s watching.**  
  Subhead: Precise Walt Disney World dining watches. SMS to the right person when a matching reservation newly opens.

## Visual system (locked)

- **Type:** Functional and warm. Headlines in something like Literata or Newsreader *sparingly*, or a humanist sans (Figtree / Source Sans 3) throughout. Prefer one family if in doubt.
- **Color:** Elevated current product: ink `#1A1A2E`, trustworthy blue `#1A56DB`, health green `#276749`, paper `#F7F8FC`, cards white. You may refine, do not abandon this family.
- **Layout:** Clear hierarchy. Create Watch is the primary panel. Status is a real control in the header, not an afterthought. Cards with visible criteria.
- **Motion:** None required. Focus rings and hover only.
- **Imagery:** None required. If used, simple line diagrams of “watch → poll → SMS,” not photography.

## Landing emphasis

Show the actual job immediately: a stylized create-watch + a health line + an owner switch. FAQ should kill the dashboard misconception. Trust strip: owner-scoped, new openings only, 10-minute poll, SMS consent.

## Logged-in emphasis

This should be the most *usable* create-watch of the three. Calendar-first dates, chips, Done, manual entry secondary. Meal checkboxes, time window, unchecked SMS consent. Watch cards list restaurant, dates, party, meal/window, owner. Empty state: “No watches yet. Create one above.” Status distinguishes healthy / needs attention / stale.

## Risk to watch (do not fall into it)

Can still feel like an admin tool. Elevate spacing, type, and empty states until it feels like a consumer product that happens to be precise.
