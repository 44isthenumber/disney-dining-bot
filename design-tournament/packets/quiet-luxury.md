# Team packet — Quiet Luxury

**Slug:** `quiet-luxury`  
**Branch:** `cursor/vision-quiet-luxury-1361`  
**Folder:** `design-tournament/quiet-luxury/`

Read [`../BRIEF.md`](../BRIEF.md) and [`../RUBRIC.md`](../RUBRIC.md) first. Honor every locked constraint. This packet is the **only** art direction you may use.

## Bet

Editorial travel. Calm. “Your table is handled.”

The guest is not hunting. They already planned the trip. They need to believe a quiet, precise watch is running while they pack, fly, and walk the parks.

## Voice

- Short sentences. No hype.
- Prefer: “We watch. You get a text.”
- Avoid: “Never miss,” “guaranteed,” “snag,” “score a table,” emoji in headlines.
- Headline direction (you may rewrite, keep the temperature):  
  **The table is being watched.**  
  Subhead: SMS when a matching Walt Disney World dining reservation opens — not a feed of every open time.

## Visual system (locked)

- **Type:** A refined serif for headlines (e.g. Fraunces or Playfair Display) + a quiet sans for UI (e.g. Source Serif is headlines only — UI must stay sans: Source Sans 3, or system-ui). Generous tracking on small caps labels.
- **Color:** Dusk ink `#1C1917`, warm paper `#F6F1E8`, gold line `#B0894A`, deep moss `#3F5C4A` for success/health. Almost no saturated blue.
- **Layout:** Lots of air. Max content width ~720px for reading, wider for the app chrome. Hairline rules, not heavy cards.
- **Motion:** Almost none. Soft fade if you must. No bounce, no confetti.
- **Imagery:** Abstract dining atmosphere — linen, candle warmth, plate rim as CSS/SVG. No park photography, no characters, no castle.

## Landing emphasis

Hero should feel like a well-set table at dusk, not a SaaS screenshot. Trust strip as a quiet list, not three neon icons. Sample SMS as a typeset message, not a flashy phone bezel unless it stays restrained.

## Logged-in emphasis

Create Watch should feel like filling a reservation card, not an admin form. Health is a single calm line: “Last checked 10:42 PM.” Owner chip is a name, not a badge farm.

## Risk to watch (do not fall into it)

Can feel like a hotel brand, not an alert product. The SMS moment and the watch criteria must still be obvious in the first screen.
