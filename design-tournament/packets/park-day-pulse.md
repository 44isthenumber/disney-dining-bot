Read [`../BRIEF.md`](../BRIEF.md) and [`../RUBRIC.md`](../RUBRIC.md) first. Honor every locked constraint. This packet is the **only** art direction you may use.

## Bet

Urgency without panic. The SMS moment is the hero.

The guest is on a park day, phone in pocket, kids already in the stroller. When Be Our Guest opens at 7:15, they need to feel the tap *now* — then book. The landing should make that moment visceral without looking like a sneaker-drop app.

## Voice

- Direct, present tense, time-aware.
- Prefer: “When it opens, you know.” / “Alert me.” / “Party of 4 · Dinner · 7:00–8:00.”
- Avoid: luxury-magazine tone, “curated,” “bespoke,” “effortless hospitality.”
- Headline direction (you may rewrite, keep the temperature):  
  **The opening text, not the hunt.**  
  Subhead: We check Walt Disney World dining every 10 minutes. You get an SMS only when a matching table newly opens.

## Visual system (locked)

- **Type:** Tight grotesque. Headlines in something like Syne, Outfit, or Archivo Black. Body in a readable grotesque (DM Sans or system-ui). High contrast. No serifs.
- **Color:** Night navy `#0B1220`, signal lime `#C8F31E` for the primary action only, hot coral `#FF5A36` for the SMS unread dot / “new opening,” white cards on dark. Do not use Disney purple or official blue.
- **Layout:** Dense but aligned. Phone mock of the SMS is the hero visual. Time windows as pill UI. Big tap targets.
- **Motion:** One restrained pulse on the SMS unread dot. No fireworks, no shake.
- **Imagery:** UI chrome and a phone frame. No characters, no castle, no park maps.

## Landing emphasis

Lead with the shared SMS fixture in the hero, then the three locked steps. Trust strip uses the four locked labels (not fake numbers). Sign-in is `#signin`.

## Logged-in emphasis

Create Watch should feel fast on a phone: large Choose Dates, meal pills that multi-select (unchecked = Any meal), time window, consent, Create Watch. Health is a live-feeling status: green = last checked recently. Owner (Craig vs Jessica) is unmissable because the wrong phone is a failed trip.

## Risk to watch (do not fall into it)

Can feel spammy or like a drop-alert app. Keep consent, STOP language, and “new matching opening” (not “every open table”) in the hero.
